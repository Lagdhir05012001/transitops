# Business Context

TransitOps is a centralized platform to manage the full lifecycle of transport operations for logistics companies that currently rely on spreadsheets and manual processes. The platform reduces scheduling conflicts, improves vehicle utilization, automates maintenance workflows, enforces compliance, and provides financial and operational insights.

## Key Goals
- Digitize vehicle, driver, dispatch, maintenance, and expense management.
- Enforce mandatory business rules to prevent invalid dispatches.
- Provide dashboards and reports for operational KPIs and cost analysis.
